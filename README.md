Solara Bay: Neon Tide — an original 3D open-world crime game built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC. Explore a neon coastal city at dusk in third person, on foot and by car: steal cars, drift for cash, outrun the SBPD (cruisers, foot cops and a police helicopter), and run six story missions with dialogue for the fixer Marisol Vega. Six weapons, a wanted system, day-night lighting, traffic and pedestrians, and automatic saves. No Rockstar Games names, characters, art or assets — the city, story, vehicles and music are all original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step — every file the game loads lives under `game/`, including the vendored Three.js.

WASD to move and drive, mouse to look, click to shoot, E to enter or steal a car, F to punch, Shift to sprint or boost nitro, Space to handbrake, 1–6 to switch weapons, R to reload, Esc to pause, M to mute. Your progress saves automatically after each mission.
